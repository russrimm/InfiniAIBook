# Code of Conduct

InfiniAIBook follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@russrimm](https://github.com/russrimm), privately
through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break
this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor,
following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
